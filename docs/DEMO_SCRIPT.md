# TowerIQ Interview Demo

1. Open the Power BI Executive Overview and explain revenue, tenancy, risk and regional KPIs.
2. Move to Tower Risk and investigate a specific tower.
3. Show Forecast & Revenue-at-Risk.
4. Show Data Quality and explain why bad rows are audited rather than silently discarded.
5. Open the Investigation Console and ask:
   - Why did tower T0148 decline?
   - Which towers are declining?
   - How are the regions performing?
   - What is the current data quality status?
6. Explain that the LLM receives an evidence packet, uses read-only analytics, and must distinguish association from causation.
