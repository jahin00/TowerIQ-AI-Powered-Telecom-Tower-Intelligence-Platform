# Portfolio Story

TowerIQ is an AI-powered telecom tower intelligence platform demonstrating an end-to-end analytics workflow.

It starts with intentionally messy operational data, applies data-quality controls and SQL transformations, performs statistical analysis and anomaly screening, forecasts revenue, prepares a Power BI semantic model, and adds an evidence-grounded natural-language investigation layer.

The central engineering idea is separation of concerns: deterministic analytics produce the evidence; the LLM summarizes and explains that evidence downstream.

The dataset is synthetic. Risk scores, thresholds and forecast classifications are project-defined analytical assumptions, not telecom-industry standards.
